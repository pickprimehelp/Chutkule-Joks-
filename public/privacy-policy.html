1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Chutkule & Vichar (चुटकुले और विचार) ("we", "our", or "the App") handles information when you use our Android mobile application and web app. By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect & Local Storage

  • No Personal Account Data: You can browse all jokes, quotes, shayari, and status cards without creating an account or providing personal details.
  • On-Device Local Preferences: Your saved favorites, theme mode (Light / Dark / AMOLED), and notification preferences are stored strictly locally on your device.
  • No Contacts or Location Access: The App does not access or collect your contacts, SMS, call logs, microphone, camera, or precise GPS location.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep the App free for all users, we integrate Google AdMob (provided by Google LLC) to display Banner, Interstitial, Native, and user-initiated Rewarded Video advertisements.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect and process limited device and diagnostic information, such as:

  • Google Advertising ID (AD_ID) and device identifiers
  • Approximate location derived from IP address
  • App interaction events, ad impressions, clicks, and crash/performance diagnostics

You can review Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from partner apps here:
https://policies.google.com/privacy
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

Users can reset or opt out of personalized ads at any time via Android Device Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID / Opt out of Ads Personalization.

4. App Permissions

  • Internet & Network State (INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to load advertisements and optional web resources.
  • Advertising ID (com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID): Used by Google AdMob for ad delivery, frequency capping, and fraud/invalid-activity prevention.
  • Storage / File Save (when saving status cards): Used only when you actively tap Download to save a generated PNG status card image to your device (Documents/ChutkuleVichar/) or share it via Android's system share sheet.
  • Notifications (optional): Used only if you enable daily quote reminders.

5. Sharing Content (WhatsApp & Social Apps)

When you tap Share to send a status card image to WhatsApp or another app, the generated image is passed directly through your device's native share sheet. We do not track or store your private messages or shared recipients.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Google Play Families Policy)

Chutkule & Vichar is intended for a general audience (ages 13 and above) and is family-friendly. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us immediately so we can assist.

7. Data Security & Retention

Because we do not operate backend user databases, no personal user profiles are stored on our servers. Any diagnostic or advertising identifiers processed by Google AdMob are governed by Google's retention and encryption standards.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised Effective Date.
